# Site cohesion audit — 2026-09-09

Measured against **production** (krystalbrookcoterie.com at `94d56ec`) by headless
Chrome: twelve routes at 1440 and 390 wide, every visible heading's computed size /
weight / tracking / colour, every `.type-meta` variant, every painted text and
background colour, every flare touch, every onyx block, every section's padding,
CTAs, hidden-above-the-fold elements, image alt, horizontal overflow, tap targets, and
17 internal links. Findings are numbered; the plan at the end groups them by who
decides. Nothing here has been changed yet.

## What is already cohesive (so it isn't re-litigated)

- **One typeface.** Every text node on every route is PP Neue Montreal (`__neueMontreal`). No fallback leaks.
- **One meta register.** Every eyebrow, tag, label and index is `13px / 600 / +0.13em / uppercase`; the only other variant is the 11px word inside the cursor. Colours: ink, ink/70, milk, milk/60, flare-deep for flags — all tokens.
- **Colours are 100% tokens.** Painted text: ink, ink/70, ink/25 (marquee ticks), milk, milk/60, cherry, deep, and ink/9 (the giant section marker). Painted backgrounds: milk, onyx, white, ink (buttons), cherry (hairlines), milk/25 (chrome dots). Nothing off-system anywhere.
- **Section rhythm is one number.** `py-section` = 120/120 on every content section; page heroes 160/80; first content section after a hero 0/120. No stray paddings.
- **Heading ladder holds on most pages.** Inner-page h1 = 84px (`display`), legal h1 = 68 (`lg`), home h1 = 36 (`sm`, by design); section h2 = 44 (`md`); closing CTA h2 = 84 (`xl`, the moment). Display weight 300 everywhere; Light is rendering, not falling back.
- **Motion is one recipe.** Text reveals (`revealText`) and media slides on the same clock across all routes; the flood is on every row/card that links; ArrowLink's wipe; the cursor's `open` only on frames.
- **Hygiene.** 0 broken internal links (17 checked), 0 images without alt, 0 horizontal overflow at 390, an h1 on every route, nothing content-bearing hidden above the fold, no console errors.

## Findings

### A · Rule breaks — the system says one thing, the page does another

1. **Flare budget exceeded on three pages.** The rule is one or two decorative touches per page. `/about` spends **four** accent words (blend in · own · distinction · your). `/work` spends **three** (one cherry phrase in each card descriptor). `/work/glowtoure` draws **six cherry ticks** in the Scope list on top of "worth". Everywhere else is on budget (hero word + closing word; index numbers and arrows are the functional carve-outs).
2. **Two-tier type broken on `/process`.** "Five phases, in order." renders at 68px (`lg`) — every other secondary heading on the site is 44 (`md`). One prop.
3. **A third small-text style on `/work`.** The card meta line ("01 · Beauty · Spray tanning", "In production") is `12px / lowercase / +0.06em` — not the meta register, and not `IndexMeta`, which every other counted sequence on the site uses.
4. **Uneven heading outline.** Some section labels are real `h2`s set as 13px meta ("What it covers", "Add-ons", home's "Ways in"); others are eyebrow `<p>`s with no heading at all (service pages: "Who it's for", "How it runs", "The work", "Questions", "Alongside"). Sighted users see one thing; the document outline says another. `/work` also jumps h1 → h3.
5. **Five flare-deep numerals in the service-page phases row.** Not `IndexMeta` (the carve-out), just coloured numbers — with the hero accent and the closing accent the page reads busier than the rule intends.
6. **The `/work` frames carry a "View →" hover label inside the frame** while the cursor already says `open` over it — two CTA vocabularies on one target; the rule is that nothing but the frame gets a cursor word and ArrowLink draws its own arrow.

### B · Structure and rhythm — cohesive on paper, not on the page

7. **The dark ending is a slab.** On every inner page the closing CTA (onyx, 120 top / 56 bottom) sits directly on the footer (onyx, 80 top): ~900px of continuous black with two padding scales inside it. The "one or two dark moments" read as one heavy one, and the home page ends differently (closing line on milk, footer the only dark). Either the closing CTA and the footer become **one composed dark ending** (CTA line + button, a milk/15 hairline, then the footer columns, single padding), or every page adopts the home pattern (closing on milk, footer dark). Decision needed; I recommend the composed ending.
8. **The case study's hero shows the wrong picture.** `/work/glowtoure` frames the OG logo card (`og-glowtoure.webp`) while every other frame of Glowtoure on the site shows the actual site (the video poster / recording). The case study should show the site.
9. **The case study's results row "Stack" duplicates the meta column** and wraps into five lines at display size ("Next.js · Tailwind / CSS · Lenis / · Pangram / Pangram / type · Vercel"). Drop the row; keep the three measured figures.
10. **Home has dead milk after the landing.** The landing wrapper holds 195svh, then the frame slot is `min-h-100svh` centred — on desktop about half a viewport of nothing between the name settling and the frame arriving; on a phone the 16:9 frame is 219px tall centred in an 844px slot, so the whole page is mostly empty (5,517px long, a third of it blank). The hold should stay; the slot should not be full-height on small screens and should sit at the top of its space.
11. **Eyebrow-only sections open with too much air.** `SectionShell` places 64px (`mt-16`) between its header and its children regardless of whether a heading exists; for eyebrow-only sections (all of the service page) that is a 13px label floating over an 80px gap.
12. **The giant section marker clips.** "Commission" renders as "ommissi.", "Services" as "Service", "Websites" as "Websit" — the label is sized for five-letter words. Either size it to fit (font-size from label length against the viewport height) or drop it for labels over seven characters.
13. **Home "Ways in" rows on a phone orphan the arrow** on its own third line under the name and the full name. The arrow belongs on the name's line.
14. **Small tap targets.** Footer links are 18px tall on ~28px pitch; "← All work" is 15px. Below the 24px minimum; a touch of vertical padding fixes all of them.

### C · Content that reads unfinished on the live site

15. **`/about` shows "Portrait — forthcoming"** as a dark placeholder card. On a studio site that sells finish, an unfinished card is the loudest thing on the page. Either a real portrait or remove the frame and let the text run full measure until one exists.
16. **`/journal` is five unlinked "forthcoming" titles**, and Journal is in the Menu and the footer. An empty journal in the nav is a promise the site can't keep yet. Hide it from nav and footer (keep the route and sitemap entry) until the first piece exists.
17. **`/privacy` and `/terms` end with "This is a working summary pending final review by counsel before launch."** — live, on the public site. Remove the line or get the review.
18. **Every line of copy is still a working default** (`docs/kbc-copy.md`), and the deck's ten inconsistencies are still live — "we" on `/process` vs "I" on `/about`, "inquiry" on the form button vs "enquiry" everywhere else, "48 hours" in the thank-you vs "two business days" on the page, the same closing line on two pages.

### D · Open decisions carried from the palette work

19. **The scrolled header over a dark band** reads as a pale strip (milk/90 over onyx).
20. **The cursor turns mint** over a cherry-flooded row (the difference blend doing its job). Accepted so far; still worth a deliberate yes or no.
