## Positioning

A KBC site is owned outright — it never holds the client hostage, and that is the
point. But owning is a house, not a serviced apartment: it performs best when it's
maintained. Care Plans are property management for a digital flagship. Offered at
handover on every engagement; highest lifetime value in the studio.

Each tier answers a different founder question:
**Essential** — "is it safe?" · **Growth** — "is it current?" · **Partner** — "is it working for me?"

---

## Essential — $175 / month · "In good condition"

The site stays fast, secure, and online. For the brand that updates rarely and
wants zero technical worry.

- Hosting & platform oversight — Vercel, deploys, DNS/SSL monitoring
- Monthly dependency + security updates, redeployed and verified
- Uptime + error monitoring
- Monthly performance check (Core Web Vitals held at launch standard)
- Monthly test of the money paths — enquiry form, booking embed, email delivery
- CMS content backups
- Up to **30 minutes** of content edits monthly (text/image swaps)
- Email support, 48-hour response
- Annual "state of the site" review

## Growth — $450 / month · "Evolving"

Everything in Essential, plus the site keeps pace with the brand. For the business
that runs seasons, offers, and launches.

- Up to **3 hours** of content + design changes monthly — new sections, seasonal
  refreshes, gallery updates, offer/pricing changes
- Monthly analytics + Core Web Vitals report with plain-English recommendations
- SEO health monitoring — Search Console, metadata upkeep, broken links
- Small conversion iterations (CTA copy, layout nudges) as data suggests
- Priority support, next business day
- Quarterly 30-minute strategy call

## Partner — $950 / month · "Retained studio"

Everything in Growth, plus KBC functions as the brand's standing design +
engineering partner.

- Up to **8 hours** of design + development monthly — new pages, campaign landing
  pages, features, email templates
- Same-day priority response
- Monthly 30-minute strategy call
- Campaign support — launches planned and shipped, not squeezed in
- First-in-line scheduling for larger projects + preferred rate *(suggested: 10% off)*
  on out-of-scope work
- Proactive roadmap — recommendations arrive without being asked

## House rules (all tiers)

- Hours don't roll over; larger work is quoted separately before it begins.
- Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. No lock-in — lock-in is what KBC sells against.
- Month-to-month; annual prepay discount optional *(suggested: 10%)*.
- Plans begin at launch/handover; can be added later after a paid site health review
  *(suggested: $250, credited to the first month)* so the studio never adopts an
  unknown state blind.

## Declining a plan — what ownership requires

Stated warmly and plainly at handover (and in the handover doc). The site is
genuinely low-maintenance — that's the craft — but responsible ownership includes:

1. **Domain renewal.** If it lapses, the site *and email* go dark. ~$15/yr;
   turn autorenew on today.
2. **Hosting account health.** Keep the Vercel account in good standing; protect the
   login with 2FA; don't delete the project.
3. **Security updates.** The design doesn't rot, but frameworks publish security
   patches. Dependencies should be updated and redeployed at least quarterly by
   someone comfortable doing it. Skipped for years, upgrade debt compounds into a
   paid project.
4. **Test the money paths monthly.** Submit the enquiry form; confirm the booking
   embed loads and the email arrives. Deliverability (DNS records) and third-party
   scripts break silently.
5. **Image discipline.** Photos uploaded through the CMS should be reasonably sized —
   8 MB phone photos will quietly destroy the performance scores the site launched
   with.
6. **Backups.** The code is delivered in git — keep the repository safe. Export CMS
   content periodically.
7. **Content freshness.** Hours, prices, services, and policies kept current — stale
   content erodes trust and rankings. Glance at Google Search Console monthly for
   errors.
8. **Legal upkeep.** Privacy/Terms stay accurate as tools and laws change.
9. **When something breaks or needs changing:** ad-hoc work is billed at the studio
   hourly rate *(suggested: $150/hr, 1-hour minimum)*, scheduled **at studio
   availability** — Care Plan clients are always first in the queue.

*The pitch, in one line: you own the house either way — a Care Plan just means the
studio that built it keeps the keys to the boiler room, so you never have to learn
where it is.*
