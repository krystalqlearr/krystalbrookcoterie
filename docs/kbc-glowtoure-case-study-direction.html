The brief

Luxury spray tanning lives in a crowded, discount-led corner of Instagram. Glowtoure's service was elevated; its digital presence had to make that obvious in five seconds, before a single price was seen.

The site had to do two jobs at once: signal genuine luxury, and make booking a mobile appointment effortless from a phone, where nearly every client arrives.

Service moment
The approach
A dark editorial system, engineered for the thumb.
01
Positioning first
A restrained, dark-led identity that reads as a boutique service, not a side hustle — luxury conveyed through space and typography, not gloss.
02
Custom-coded
Hand-built in Next.js on Vercel — no template, no page builder. Fast, owned, and extensible as the brand grows.
03
Booking, effortless
A mobile-first flow that turns a phone tap into a confirmed appointment, with the booking system woven into the design rather than bolted on.
