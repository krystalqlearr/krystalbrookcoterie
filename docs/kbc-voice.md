# KBC — Brand Voice & Copy System

> The visual identity says *fashion editorial meets modern creative studio*. The copy
> must say: **we understand image, positioning, and desire — and we also understand
> what makes a premium business convert.** This is the source of truth for tone and
> the canonical page copy. Adopted 2026-07 (supersedes the earlier dev-forward copy).

## The disconnect we fixed
The old copy read like a technically sophisticated web-development agency ("hand-built
in code," "the same stack the largest technology companies run," "advanced interaction,
deeper architecture," "custom systems and engineering"). Those points matter — but they
**support** the sale, they don't lead it. The ideal client is buying **perception,
authority, trust, desirability, and growth**: she wants her business to look established,
distinctive, and worth the price before she reads the service menu.

## The voice
Editorial · assured · fashion-conscious · commercially intelligent · selective ·
slightly provocative. A luxury creative house that happens to be exceptionally capable
at strategy and development.

**Not:** overly feminine, flowery, corporate, or filled with interchangeable luxury
language. Avoid using **elevated, bespoke, intentional, curated, timeless** without
saying anything specific.

**Underlying message:** *We create digital identities for brands that expect to be
noticed, trusted, and chosen.*

## Rules
1. Lead with the outcome (perception, authority, desire, conversion); tech is a proof
   point kept to the FAQ and case-study depth — never the headline.
2. Frame work as the **shift created**, not the deliverable ("a clinical brand,
   repositioned as a modern authority"), with capabilities listed beneath
   (Brand Strategy · Web Design · Development · Copy Direction).
3. Nomenclature: an **engagement / commission**, not a "package." Nav CTA = "Commission."
4. Never name competitors. Confidence doesn't name the cheap option.
5. Headings are sentence case, end in a period, and are set LIGHT (300) in the one
   grotesk — there is no serif and no uppercase display (both retired 2026-08; the
   2026-09 Round 2 pivot went lighter still). Meta labels are the only uppercase.
6. Never mention AI anywhere except the single "Do you use AI?" FAQ on /services
   (CLAUDE.md, hard rule).
7. **Fewest true declarative words** (2026-09). The site no longer argues for its
   own quality; it has it. No adjective that sells. The homepage's studio line is
   the `h1` and must contain "websites" or "web design". The wordmark is lowercase.
