# KBC Founding Client Program — Offer Sheet

> Internal source of truth + the basis for outreach one-pagers and proposals.
> Numbers marked *(suggested)* are recommendations — adjust before first send.

---

## The offer

Full **Signature-tier scope — a $9,800 engagement — for $2,800**, in exchange for
case-study rights. Limited to the **first three** founding clients; the program closes
when three agreements are signed or on **[set date — suggest 90 days from first
outreach]**, whichever comes first.

This is not a discount for the asking — it is an exchange. The founding client gets
the full KBC standard at a fraction of its price; the studio gets the published proof
that lets it charge full price from client four onward. Both sides should feel they
got the better end.

## Eligibility

- Founder-led beauty, med-spa, wellness, bridal, or luxury lifestyle brand.
- An existing brand identity (logo + basic identity). This engagement designs and
  builds the website; it does not create the brand.
- One decision-maker, available for feedback within three business days at review
  milestones.
- Comfortable with the case-study exchange below.

## What's included — Signature scope

- **Art direction & design system** — bespoke to the brand; no theme, no template.
- **Up to 6 custom-designed pages/templates**, hand-built in code (Next.js · Tailwind ·
  Vercel). A CMS-driven template (e.g., a treatment/service detail) counts as one
  design and can power unlimited entries.
- **Privacy, Terms, and a custom 404** included — they don't count against the six.
- **CMS where it earns its place** — self-serve content updates where genuinely useful.
- **Editorial motion & interaction** — the reveals, transitions, and pacing of the
  KBC standard.
- **Embedded booking/scheduling** — integration of the client's existing scheduler
  (Square, Acuity, Vagaro, etc.).
- **Foundational SEO** — metadata, Open Graph, structured data, sitemap, and
  performance engineered for Core Web Vitals.
- **Accessibility** — WCAG 2.1 AA.
- **An art-directed shot list** for the client's photographer, so the imagery matches
  the design intent.
- **Launch + full handover** — deployed to the client's own infrastructure. The
  client owns the code, the design system, and the deployment outright.

## Timeline

**6–8 weeks from contract signing to delivery.** The clock holds when content and
assets arrive by the agreed dates (see "What I need from you"); late content pauses
the timeline, it doesn't compress the quality.

## Revisions

**Two consolidated revision rounds** are included — one at design, one at build.
Feedback arrives as a single consolidated document per round from the decision-maker.
Additional rounds available at the founding add-on rate below. (Day-to-day: individual
revisions inside a round turn around in hours, not weeks.)

## Investment & payment

- **$2,800** — 50% ($1,400) to book the slot and start date; 50% due at launch
  approval, before go-live and handover.
- Third-party costs are the client's own (domain ≈ $15/yr; hosting on Vercel's free
  tier is typically $0). Accounts are created in the client's name — they own
  everything from day one.

## What I need from you (client responsibilities)

1. **One decision-maker.** Feedback comes from one voice, consolidated.
2. **Feedback within 3 business days** at each review milestone.
3. **Content by the agreed date** — final copy (or drafts, if copy polish is added)
   and all imagery/brand assets before build start. Late content pauses the timeline.
4. **Brand assets** — logo files, any brand guidelines, licensed fonts.
5. **Access** — domain registrar (or DNS delegation) and any existing accounts needed
   for integration.
6. **The case-study exchange** — analytics access (90 days), the 30-minute interview,
   a testimonial within 14 days of launch, and the footer credit.
7. **Payment on schedule** — the balance clears before go-live and handover.

## Why this works (internal note)

The $2,800 is funded by what the case study buys: published proof, real performance
numbers, a testimonial, and a live credit link in front of exactly the audience KBC
sells to. Hold the cap at three, hold the expiry date, and never extend the rate
quietly — the price anchors as an exception, not a negotiable rate.
