Before you bookRead once, and we're set

The short version.

Four things that keep every appointment on time and every set safe. The booking flow repeats them, so nothing is a surprise.

  • Deposit

    A deposit holds your appointment and comes off your total. Paid at booking, by card. It protects your time as much as mine.

    At booking

  • Cancellations

    Move or cancel up to 24 hours ahead and your deposit carries over. Inside 24 hours it is kept. Life happens, so just let me know early.

    24 hours

  • Arrive ready

    Clean, makeup-free lashes, and skip the coffee right before. Your set goes on faster and lasts longer.

    Day of

  • First time here

    New clients complete a short intake with patch-test questions. Sensitive eyes get a patch test 48 hours ahead. I would rather be careful with you.

    First visit
